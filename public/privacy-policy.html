Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 28, 2026

Keystone Digital LLC ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our Decision Tarot mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

  • Purchase Information: When you make an in-app purchase, we receive transaction information from Google Play Store, including purchase date, product ID, and transaction status. We do not store your credit card information.
  • User Identifier: We generate an anonymous user ID to associate your purchase with your device. This ID is not linked to your personal identity.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: We collect anonymous usage statistics through Google Analytics 4, including app opens, screen views, and feature usage. This data is aggregated and cannot identify you personally.
  • Device Information: We may collect information about your device type, operating system version, and app version for analytics and debugging purposes.
  • Local Storage: The App stores your reading history and preferences locally on your device. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to back it up.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and verify in-app purchases
  • Unlock premium features after purchase
  • Save your reading history locally on your device
  • Analyze app usage to improve functionality
  • Debug technical issues and crashes
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

3.1 Google AdMob (Advertising)

Starting with version 5.1.0, the App displays banner advertisements through Google AdMob in the free tier. AdMob and its partners may collect and process the following to serve relevant ads: device identifiers, approximate location (coarse, non-GPS), app interaction data, and diagnostic information. Users in the EEA, UK, and applicable US states are shown a consent form before ads load and may withdraw consent at any time via device privacy settings or by upgrading to Premium, which removes all ads. Google's privacy policy and how it processes advertising data: policies.google.com/privacy. You may also review Google's ad personalization opt-outs at adssettings.google.com.

3.2 Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Google Play Billing. Purchase transactions and history are handled by Google Play Billing (and its supporting infrastructure). Google's use of data is governed by the Google Play Terms of Service: play.google.com/about/play-terms

3.3 AI Reading Generation

When you request an AI-written reading with Premium, your question text and the drawn cards' public meanings are sent to our server, which calls a large language model (Groq, using an open-weights model) to generate your personalized reading. Your question text is processed to produce the reading and is not used to train models, is not combined with other personal information, and is not retained on our server beyond the request. Groq's privacy policy: groq.com/privacy-policy. If you do not use the AI reading feature, nothing is transmitted for that feature. Standard (template) readings are generated entirely on your device with no network call.

3.4 Other Third-Party Services

The App may use additional services that may collect information from time to time as features evolve; this section will be updated accordingly with notice as described in Section 8.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

The App does not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. Advertising partners (as described in Section 3.1) act as independent controllers of their own data collection; we do not sell ad data. We may share information with:

  • Advertising (AdMob): Device identifiers, coarse location, app interaction and diagnostic data as described in Section 3.1, collected by Google for ad delivery
  • AI Reading Processing (Groq): Your question text and card meanings, only when you request an AI-written reading, as described in Section 3.3
  • Purchase Processing (Google Play Billing): Transaction handling as described in Section 3.2
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law, regulation, or legal process
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

5. Data Retention

We retain your purchase information for as long as necessary to provide you with the App's premium features. Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' retention policies (typically 14 months by default). You can request deletion of your data by contacting us.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Access to your personal information
  • Correction of inaccurate information
  • Deletion of your information
  • Opt-out of data collection (by uninstalling the App)

To exercise these rights, please contact us at the email address below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a child under 13, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy in the App and updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this policy. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
